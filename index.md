# {{ title }}

I hold a postgraduate degree from IIT Delhi and spent six to seven years working in multinational corporate environments. During my years in the United States, I began teaching at engineering colleges — a transition that allowed me to balance professional engagement with raising my children.

Teaching soon became more than a profession; it became a calling.

While guiding my son through mathematics, I rediscovered the depth and beauty of problem solving. He earned a perfect score of 25/25 in AMC 8 in Grade 6, qualified for AIME in Grade 7, and narrowly missed MOP qualification by a single mark. Inspired by this journey, I immersed myself deeply in mathematics and physics, dedicating four to five hours daily to rigorous study alongside my children.

Under my mentorship, both of my children gained admission to some of the world’s most prestigious institutions, including MIT, Harvard University, University of Oxford, University of Cambridge, and IIT.

Even after they began their independent journeys, my pursuit of mathematics continued with the same intensity. Today, I devote myself fully to teaching Olympiad mathematics to students across the world.

My areas of focus include AMC 8, AMC 10, AMC 12, AIME, USAJMO, IOQM, RMO, and INMO, with a growing specialization in geometry — the field where logic meets elegance.

I believe mathematics is not merely a subject to be learned, but a way of thinking to be cultivated.
